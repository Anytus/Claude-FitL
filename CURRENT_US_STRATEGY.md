# Current US strategy

Advice distilled from five games against the Tru'ng bots. It records what has
worked and what has cost games; it is **not** a set of rules, and you are free
to play differently when the board says so. The rules are in
`RULES_LEARNED.md` and on the cards.

Record so far: lost to the VC at the 3rd Coup (game 1); won at the 3rd Coup
by 4, 8 and 10 (games 2, 3, 5) and at the 2nd Coup by 6 and 2 (games 4 and 6).

**What the score is made of.** About 21 to 23 points sit in Available from
the start; the rest has to be Support on the map. The game 1 loss came from
keeping the score in the box and a few Cities, with nothing on the map that
could stop the VC. Saigon is the first purchase in every game (pop 6, and
Govern cannot reach it); pop-2 spaces come next.

**Victory is strictly above 50, checked first in the Coup.** Twice the US sat
on exactly 50 at a Victory check and lost a whole campaign to it. Nothing the
Coup itself does counts for that check. Know the margin you need two cards
before a Coup card can appear, and spend an action buying it. Because the
highest score above 0 wins, a point of your own counts against every rival at
once. Check all three rivals each card: one NVA March can cross its 18.

**Prepare the Coup Support phase before the Coup card turns.** Spaces with
COIN Control, US Troops and ARVN Police below Active Support are pacified in
that phase from ARVN's fresh income. Built deliberately, they paid +6 and +14
in the last two games; Limited Ops with nothing better to do are the cheap
way to set them up.

**ARVN's Resources are your pacification budget.** You spend only what is
above Econ, and the bot can drain it on its own turn. When there is room to
pacify, pacify now; later there may be none.

**Advise every action you can.** Irregular/Ranger removal costs nothing,
reaches Underground Guerrillas and undefended Bases, and breaks Controls. Keep
Underground Irregulars and Rangers where the enemy is, spread across spaces
rather than stacked next to large NVA stacks. A Base removed from a faction
with none in Available is close to permanent.

**Troops are tools, not shields.** Terror and Agitate need an enemy piece in
the space, not a Control margin, so garrisons do not protect Support; removing
the Guerrilla does. Air Lift moves Troops map to map at no score cost. Every
piece moved out of Available costs a point, so give it a job the box cannot
do. Small garrisons beside insurgent stacks are lost; a Control won by a tie
lasts one bot action, so buy a real margin or remove pieces instead.

**Govern is the steadiest leak in the US score.** It reaches Support spaces
holding ARVN cubes outside Saigon. Support held by US pieces alone, or in
Saigon, is safe from it.

**Watch the VC's Agitate Total.** Tax looks like a gift of Support but banks
points that are spent after the Victory check, where nothing can answer them.
Deal with Taxed spaces before the Coup.

**Turn order is the only guarantee.** Pass only when the next card's printed
order makes your action certain; passes that relied on a bot choosing to act
failed. An Op without a Special Activity closes the Event to everyone behind
you. On the last event card before a Coup, acting to end the card denies a
rival its turn before the Victory check. When your action cannot end the card,
an Op without a Special Activity holds the next faction to one space.

**Events.** Weigh each Event against what an Op with a Special Activity would
build. Taking Out-of-Play US pieces onto the map is free; taking Available
pieces costs a point each. A pivotal deletes the **current** card, not the one
on deck: read which is which before playing one.

**Long games.** Each Coup makes the bots' pivotals likelier, and Tet
Offensive can undo a campaign in one card. At Trail 4 NVA stacks cross from
the sanctuaries in one March. Getting over the line early is worth more than
building a lead for later.
