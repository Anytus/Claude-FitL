Next-card eligibility ("tempo") test: plan (written before the run)

Change: USPolicy "tempo": d. When a Pass would leave the US first eligible on the next card
(Diag.firstNextIfPass: every faction before the US in the on-deck card's order will be
ineligible), the Pass candidate's value gets d x the next-card gain: the best of the US's
first-eligible candidates (the on-deck card's Event, every Operation with or without a Special
Activity), each tried on the board as it would be after the pass, less that board's value
(nextCardGain). When it depends on factions still to act on this card ("maybe"), half that.
Nothing changes when the next card is a Coup or the US would not be first. d = 0.9 was set
before any run (not tuned).

Arms (all fit3flat_event0 otherwise; the same build; seeds 1-800, paired;
--us-final-only --us-player; FITL_ACTION_STATS for pass decisions by next-card status):
  B   baseline: fit3flat_event0
  T   tempo 0.9 (policies/fit3flat_event0_tempo.json)
  T0  tempo 0.9 and the fixed Pass bonus (+0.226) removed (fit3flat_event0_tempo_nopass.json)

Hypotheses:
H1 (mechanism): under T the US passes more often when a pass leaves it first on the next card
   than when it does not (B: no difference, results/breadth/results.txt).
H2 (main): T wins more often than B. Expected +2 to +4 points; 800 paired games give a se of
   about 2 points.
H3 (secondary): T0 against B and against T.

Decision rule: adopt T if its paired win difference over B is positive with z >= 2; if
0 < z < 2, run seeds 801-1600 before deciding; if negative, do not adopt. The same rule for T0.

Before the run (smoke tests on 20 games, seeds 1-20, not part of the test): the first build gave
each next-card candidate its own dice, so the best of about 70 trials picked lucky rolls; fixed
to the same dice for every candidate, as in the search's own first trial. With the fix, the
smoke games passed in 90% of the decisions where the US had full options and a pass kept it
first on the next card (the LLM: 31% of 13), mostly 1-2 passes in a row. Part of that is the
rule: a US pass gives ARVN +3 Resources, the Pacify budget, and the US gets one action every
two cards either way. d was not changed.
