What US actions accomplish, the spaces open to them, and turn economy: the LLM's games
against the bot's (tools/breadth.py; output in summary.txt)

Data: the LLM's 4 games (games 7-10; fitl.Diag --saves on their saves: 131 US card
decisions, detected from the sequence of play and checked against the narration's
counts); the bot: fit3flat_event0, seeds 1-100, --us-final-only --us-player, the +6 Aid
build (FITL_ACTION_STATS: 2916 decisions; 51 US wins, 48 games reach the final Coup).
Measures per decision (Diag.actionStats, Diag.opportunity, Diag.firstNextIfPass): spaces
the action changed, and changed for the better (enemy removed or activated, Support up or
Opposition down, COIN Control gained, NVA Control broken, ARVN or Irregulars placed,
Terror removed); enemy pieces and bases removed; pieces placed; Support, US score, best
rival; the spaces where each US Operation could act before and after; whether passing
would have left the US first eligible on the next card (the on-deck card's order and who
has acted or may still act).

1. Per Op+SA the LLM did not accomplish more (mean, se):
                         LLM (60)      bot (1752)
   spaces changed        3.05 (0.14)   4.24 (0.06)
   ... for the better    2.40 (0.11)   3.45 (0.03)
   enemy removed         3.30 (0.33)   3.14 (0.06)
   COIN Control gained   0.58 (0.11)   0.67 (0.02)
   Support / US score    +1.40 (0.27)  +1.25 (0.05)
   best rival            -0.97 (0.18)  -0.94 (0.04)
   ARVN pieces placed    0.27          2.15
   The bot acts in more spaces (largely ARVN cubes placed by Train) for about the same result.
   Per game, the bot's own actions do more than the LLM's: 20.9 Op+SA a game against 15.0
   (the LLM: more Events and Limited Ops), 83 spaces changed for the better against 51,
   Support +34.7 against +26.2, US score +36.4 against +31.5, best rival -26.0 against -27.0
   (bot games reaching the final Coup).

2. Spaces open to US Operations at the US's decisions: early and in the middle of the game
   the bot has as many or more (Train 10.9 against 7.7 early, Coup Pacify 5.5 against 2.7);
   late (52+ cards seen) the LLM has about twice as many for Sweep (8.2 against 3.8) and Coup
   Pacify (2.5 against 1.2), and more for Train (8.8 against 6.6): the bot takes US pieces off
   the map in the final campaign. A single action changes the open spaces little for either
   (both use up a few).

3. Turn economy: the LLM's passing depends on whether a pass leaves it first eligible on the
   next card; the bot's does not at all.
   Pass rate:                first next card    not first
     LLM, full options        31% of 13          3% of 31
     LLM, after an Op         80% of 5           0% of 5
     LLM, after an Op+SA      36% of 11          12% of 16
     bot, full options         1% of 259          1% of 673
     bot, after an Op         64% of 100         60% of 181
     bot, after an Op+SA      39% of 176         36% of 370
   The bot's search has no notion of next-card eligibility; its passes come from the fixed
   Pass bonus and weak Limited Ops.

Caveat: the LLM sample is 4 games.
