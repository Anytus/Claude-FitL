Space-choice screening: plan (written before the run)

Question: does the search bot lose win chance in its space choices (which the
Tru'ng US routines make inside each Operation and in the Coup Support phase)?

Policy fit3flat_event0 plays the games and the playouts (--us-final-only --us-player).
Schedule: 140 games, 4 workers x 35 (seeds 920001-920035, 921001-921035, 922001-922035,
923001-923035), tools/teach_run.sh; progress in the run directory's PROGRESS.txt.
FITL_TEACHER_MODE=space, 100 playouts to the end of the game per candidate, common seeds,
per-step reseed.
- US turns: each US decision sampled with probability 0.1. The policy's pick is carried out
  once with its space picks traced; if it made any space pick with an alternative, the
  candidates are the pick as planned and up to 3 variants, each changing one space pick to
  another space that pick was offered (chosen at random among all such changes); the
  routine continues by its own priorities after the change.
- Coup rounds: sampled with probability 0.15 when no Bot is over its line; candidates the
  round as played and up to 3 variants changing one of the US Bot's Support-phase Pacify
  picks.
Expected: about 300 turn decisions and 100 Coup decisions, about 3-4 hours on 4 cores.

Measures (tools/teacher_space.py), separately for turns and Coups:
S1 held-out regret of the bot's choice against the playout-best of {as played, variants}
   (best chosen on 50 playouts, measured on the other 50, both ways): mean (se), median.
   This is the prize: what a perfect one-change chooser among these variants would gain.
S2 the same by kind of pick (the routine's first priority, e.g. "Vulnerable Base",
   "City or Province not at Active Support") and by action.
S3 mean variant minus as played: how much a random one-pick change costs on average.
Variants applied in fewer than 90% of their playouts are reported and left out.

Decision rule (stated now): if S1 for turns or Coups is +1 point a decision or more with
se under 0.5, space choice is worth building (the learned rule); if it is under +0.5,
like the menu (+0.2), it is not, and the next place to look is elsewhere.
Note that S1 includes the selection effect of picking the best of up to 4 by noisy
halves only partly: the held-out half removes the bias in the measured value but a
best-of-4 on 50 playouts still picks noise often, which pulls S1 towards 0, not away.
