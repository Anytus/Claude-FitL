Teacher screening run: plan (written before the run)

Policy fit3flat_event0 plays the games (seeds 910001+, 911001+, 912001+, 913001+;
--us-final-only --us-player) and the playouts. FITL_TEACHER_MODE=screen: each US
turn decision (not Coup-round decisions, which the Tru'ng routines make) is labelled
with probability 0.1, 125 a process, 500 in all. Candidates: the policy's pick and the
best-scoring candidates of up to 3 other kinds (Event, Pass, Train, Patrol, Sweep,
Assault, each Limited Op its own kind), with different positions. 100 playouts to the
end of the game per candidate, common seeds, per-step reseed. Expected about 4-5 hours
on 4 cores (playouts from early positions take about 0.7 s, late ones far less).
Features before the decision and after each candidate's trial are recorded for the
follow-up analysis.

Measures (the purpose is descriptive: where the decisive decisions are):
M1 held-out regret of the policy's pick: the best candidate chosen on one half of the
   playouts, measured on the other half (both ways); mean, median, and the share of
   decisions with regret above 10 and above 20 points; by the policy pick's kind, by
   first/second eligible and by game stage (cards seen 0-25, 26-51, 52-78).
M2 decisions whose candidates differ by more than 20 points (all playouts), and
   which kinds are best and worst in them.
M3 correlation of policy score differences with playout win differences.

Predictions from the pilot: mean regret about 2 points, median about 0; about 10-15%
of decisions with candidates more than 20 points apart; score/playout correlation
about 0.2.
