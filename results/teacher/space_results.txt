Not pre-registered: decisive cases. Only one decision of 386 has a variant that changes

Tru'ng priorities say. With the Failed Attempt case of the menu screening (a Coup on deck,
the pick put NVA over its line) this is the second decisive decision found, both "last
US action before a Victory check" positions, both missed because the losing outcome was
not in what the search compared.

Conclusions:
1. Like the menu, the space choices the bot makes are close to the best one-change
   alternative on average; a general space-choice rule has little to gain.
2. The Tru'ng Coup Pacify ordering looks no better than random among eligible spaces;
   whether a better one would gain anything is not settled (+0.6, se 0.6).
3. What two screenings found worth something are rare positions just before a Victory
   check, where one choice puts a rival over its line and another does not. A targeted
   guard for those (check each candidate and its space variants against the coming
   Victory check, including the Coup card's own effect) is the remaining concrete lead.
