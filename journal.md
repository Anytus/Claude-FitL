## Turn 1 — card #63 Fact Finding — before save-002
**Situation.** First card of the game and it is a Monsoon card: the Coup (#125 Nguyen Khanh) is on deck. ARVN, first eligible, took the shaded event (Cam Ranh Passive Support -> Neutral, Patronage +4), so the US is second eligible with Op + Special Activity. NVA and VC still act after me. Scores all negative (US -13, VC -8, ARVN -11, NVA -14) so nobody wins at the Coup; the Coup's Support phase is where the US can pacify, and the Commitment phase is where Available pieces move. Total Support 14, Available Troops/Bases 23.
**Options considered.** (a) Sweep/Assault against the VC guerrillas sitting on my three Highland outposts (Quang Tri, Binh Dinh, Pleiku-Darlac): the guerrillas are all Underground and Highland halves Assault, so one Op would remove little. (b) Train with Pacify in Saigon: pop 6, Passive Support, has US Troops + ARVN Police + COIN Control, so a one-level shift to Active Support is worth +6 US points at once, for 3 ARVN Resources. The Coup Support phase can then be spent on other spaces (Da Nang, Kontum) instead of Saigon. (c) Event unshaded is not available (ARVN already executed the card). Special Activity: Air Strike purely to degrade the Trail 1 -> 0 (no active guerrillas exist to hit) is the only SA with a clear payoff; Monsoon may forbid it, in which case I fall back to Advise or skip the SA.
**Plan.** Op + SA. Operation: Train. Spaces: Saigon (Pacify: Passive Support -> Active Support, 3 ARVN Resources). If the program lets US Train also place pieces in other spaces at no cost, add Pleiku-Darlac (US Base there) to place 2 Irregulars from Available (Irregulars in Available do not count for score, so it is free). Special Activity: Air Strike, degrade Trail only, no space chosen. Expected: Total Support 14 -> 20, US score -13 -> -7, ARVN Resources 30 -> 27, Trail 1 -> 0 if the strike is allowed. Available US Troops/Bases unchanged at 23.
**Rationale.** The Coup is next, so this is the last action before the Victory check and the Support phase; the highest-value single thing I can do is convert Saigon's population-6 Passive Support to Active, which is a +6 swing for 3 Resources and leaves the Coup-phase pacification budget for the pop-1 cities where I also have Troops and Police. Keeping the 21 Troops and 2 Bases in Available preserves 23 points of US score; moving them out to fight would cost score now for uncertain gains before a Coup. Degrading the Trail before the NVA get their Coup-round Trail improvement is cheap if Monsoon allows it.
**Execution.** (filled in below)
Execution (actual): Op menu -> 1 (Op), then the program printed "Sweep is prohibited [Not allowed in Monsoon]" and offered Train/Patrol/Assault. Chose Train. Selected Saigon; the placement menu offered Irregulars / Rangers / ARVN Troops/Police; I placed 0 Troops and 2 Police, and the program charged "Decrease ARVN resources by -3 to 27" — I had assumed US Train placement was free, it costs 3 ARVN Resources per space. Because of that cost I cut the plan to one more space. DEVIATION: I meant to select Pleiku-Darlac but the space menu had renumbered after Saigon was removed, so my "5" selected Quang Tri-Thua Thien. I accepted it rather than abort (same outpost-vs-VC-base situation) and placed 2 Irregulars there; that made Quang Tri COIN-controlled (COIN Control marker 39 -> 41), an unplanned bonus. Special Activity: Air Strike was allowed in Monsoon ("Max 2 spaces in Monsoon"), hit die rolled 5, I chose Degrade the trail (1 -> 0) and struck no spaces. Final Train action: Pacify Saigon, 1 level, 3 Resources (24 left), Passive -> Active Support; the program only offered one level. No rejections. After my action the program moved US and ARVN to Ineligible and asked for the next on-deck card: NVA and VC do not act on this card.
**Result.** Support marker 37 -> 43 (expected +6, got +6). ARVN Resources 30 -> 24 (I expected 27; Train placement cost 3 more). Trail 1 -> 0 as hoped. Quang Tri-Thua Thien COIN Control gained. Diff deferred until the next card number is entered.

## Coup 1 (card #125 Nguyen Khanh) — Support phase decision — before save-003
**Situation.** Victory phase: nobody wins. Resources: ARVN 24 -> 54, Econ 15, no sabotage, no casualties. US Pacification offers Da Nang and Kontum (both Neutral, pop 1, US Troops + ARVN Police, COIN Control). Saigon is already Active Support and Quang Tri has no Police.
**Options considered.** Pacify neither (save ARVN Resources for the bot); pacify one; pacify both as far as allowed. The prompt mentions "Econ is 15", which may cap spending.
**Plan.** Pacify Da Nang and Kontum, each up to 2 levels (Neutral -> Active Support), 3 Resources per level. Expected: Total Support +4 (US 43 -> 47), ARVN Resources 54 -> 42.
**Rationale.** Each level is +1 US point for 3 ARVN Resources and ARVN has 54; the bot will spend the rest anyway. Both spaces already have the Troops and Police needed and are cities, so the Support is likely to stick.
**Execution.** (below)
Execution (Support phase): Da Nang -> option "Shift 2 levels to Active Support" (-6, 48 left); Kontum -> same (-6, 42 left). No rejections. ARVN then pacified Hue and Cam Ranh to Passive (36 left); VC agitated Binh Dinh to Passive Opposition (Agitate total 0). Support + Avail US marker 43 -> 50.
**Result.** As expected: +4 US from my two spaces, plus the ARVN work on Hue/Cam Ranh took the marker to 50 (score 0; needs >0 to win).

## Coup 1 (card #125) — Commitment phase decision — before save-003
**Situation.** Redeploy: ARVN moved 1 Troop Saigon -> Pleiku-Darlac (Pleiku now COIN Control) and 4 Police Saigon -> Quang Tri. No casualties. The program offers to move up to 10 US Troops and 2 Bases among Available, COIN-controlled spaces, LoCs and Saigon. US Troops on map: Da Nang 2, Kontum 2, Saigon 2, Quang Tri 1, Binh Dinh 1, Pleiku 1. US score is exactly 0 (Support 27 + Available 23 = 50).
**Options considered.** (a) Do nothing. (b) Deploy Troops from Available to Police-only provinces (Kien Hoa, Phu Bon, Khanh Hoa, Ba Xuyen) to enable US pacification later: -1 score each now, payoff only after several Train ops. (c) Pull Troops that are doing no further work back to Available: Da Nang and Kontum are already Active Support, so their 2 Troops each only hold Control; each Troop returned is +1 US now. (d) Pull the Pleiku base to Available: +1 but gives up the only US base outside Saigon (US Train can place ARVN cubes only where a US base is) and probably Control there.
**Plan.** Move 1 Troop from Da Nang to Available and 1 Troop from Kontum to Available. Leave 1 Troop + 1 Police in each so US pacification there stays possible if Support is later knocked down, and each city keeps 2 COIN pieces for Control. Keep Saigon (pop 6 Active Support) fully garrisoned and keep the Pleiku base. Expected: Available Troops 21 -> 23, US 50 -> 52 (score +2).
**Rationale.** Under the JFK policy the US pool is the score; troops on the map earn only when they enable pacification. Da Nang and Kontum cannot be pacified further, so half their garrison is dead weight, but stripping them to a lone Police invites a two-guerrilla march to take Control. Two Troops back is the cautious version of (c). I am not deploying new troops yet: the US is Ineligible on the next card (#75) and I would rather see what the NVA and VC do with their free Cambodia Rally/March before committing.
**Execution.** (below)
Execution (Commitment): Move troops -> Da Nang -> Available box -> 1; Move troops -> Kontum -> Available box -> 1; Finished. No rejections. THEN the program applied a rule I did not know: "WITHDRAWAL: For every 2 US pieces just removed to Available, VC shifts 1 population of support by 1 level toward Active Opposition" and flipped Kontum Active Support -> Passive Support. Reset phase: Trail improved 0 -> 1, all four cylinders to Eligible (so the US IS eligible on card #75), Agitate Total rolled 3, Tru'ng deck reshuffled.
**Result.** Support + Avail US marker 50 -> 51 (score +1), not the +2 I expected: the withdrawal penalty cost one Support level. Lesson: pulling pieces to Available in Commitment is taxed at 1 Support level per 2 pieces, so it only pays in even lots and only when the VC has cheap targets to hit anyway. Coup-round diff deferred until the next card number is entered.
