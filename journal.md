## Turn 1 — card #17 Claymores — before save-000
**Plan.** Op + SA: Train Saigon (no placement) and Quang Tri (2 Irregulars); Pacify Saigon Passive→Active; Advise Irregular removal of 2 VC Guerrillas each in Pleiku-Darlac and Binh Dinh; +6 Aid.
**Why.** Saigon Active is +6 Support for 3 Resources; Advise strips the guards off two VC Bases and gives COIN Control there. Claymores unshaded (stay Eligible) is weaker than +6 and four Guerrillas.
**Execution.** Program was not running at session start; resumed from save-000 before the first answer. At the 2nd Advise, Binh Dinh was the only candidate and was auto-selected, so the seq's `Binh Dinh` answer landed on `Do you wish to add +6 Aid? (y/n)`, which re-prompted; answered `y` by send. Mechanic: Quang Tri (just trained, COIN Control) was also offered for Pacify.
**Result.** As planned: Saigon Active (+6), 4 VC Guerrillas removed, COIN Control in Quang Tri, Pleiku, Binh Dinh; Aid 21. ARVN took Claymores unshaded.
